Program

Elephant Nature

Your 3-hour day, step by step

Our most complete program, best for families. 09:00 or 14:00 · 2,700 THB adult · 1,500 THB child 4–11.

1

Walk with the elephants

Start together with a guided walk along the jungle trail. Watch the elephants graze and cool off in the water, and feed them bananas.

2

Rubber tapping & rubber sheets

See how latex is tapped from rubber trees, then help press it into a rubber sheet with the old hand-turned roller. Kids love this part.

3

Fresh coconut milk

Watch a coconut cracked open and scraped by hand, then squeezed into fresh coconut milk, the base of many Thai dishes. You’ll also see how Tom Kha Gai is made.

4

Thai lunch with a view

Finish with a meal of 5 Thai dishes and fresh fruit in the open-air dining hall: fried rice, spring rolls, stir-fried vegetables and more, with cold drinks and a cool towel waiting for you.

Walk & Feed

Make rice balls for the elephants

Our guests’ favourite. About 1.5 hours · 09:00, 11:00 or 14:00 · 1,600 THB adult · 1,000 THB child 4–11.

1

Walk with the elephants

Join the group walk along the jungle trail, watch the elephants up close and feed them bananas.

2

Prepare the fruit

Fresh bananas, watermelon and pineapple are chopped and ready on the table. The fruit changes with each session.

3

Make the rice balls

Put on gloves and mix rice and fruit together by hand, then press it into rice balls. It’s messy, simple and fun for all ages.

4

Feed the elephants

Carry your plate of rice balls to the elephants and feed them yourself. This is the moment our guests love most.

Mini Elephant Nature

A short visit with a taste of Thai life

About 1.5 hours · 09:00, 11:00 or 14:00 · 1,400 THB adult · 800 THB child 4–11.

1

Walk with the elephants

Join the group walk along the jungle trail in rubber boots. Watch the elephants graze and cool off in the water, and feed them bananas.

Rubber tapping
