Good to know

Before you go

👤Who can ride

  • Riders aged 17 and over can drive
  • Under 17s can drive only with a parent or staff member riding with them
  • Max 2 people per jet ski
  • Weight limit: 120 kg on the Yamaha 1100cc, 160 kg on the other two

⚠️Not recommended for

  • People over 70 or children under 6
  • Pregnant women
  • People with back pain or heart disease
  • People with asthma or panic disorder

🎒What to bring

  • Sunglasses and sunscreen
  • Towel
  • Dry bag
  • Waterproof phone case
  • Water shoes
