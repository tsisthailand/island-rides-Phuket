Jet Ski Tour

Choose your jet ski

A 4-hour guided jet ski tour from AA Marina, Rasada, with 9 stops in Phang Nga Bay, from Mali Island to the Mangrove Forest. Run by Phuket Jet Ski Club, a fully licensed operator. Price is per jet ski (2 people share one).

Who can ride

  • Riders aged 17 and over can drive.
  • Riders under 17 can drive only with a parent or staff member riding with them.
  • Max 2 people per jet ski. Weight limit is 120 kg on the Yamaha 1100cc and 160 kg on the other two models.

Not recommended for

  • People over 70 or children under 6
  • Pregnant women
  • People with back pain, heart disease, asthma or panic disorder

What to bring

  • Sunglasses
  • Towel
  • Dry bag
  • Waterproof phone case
  • Sunscreen
  • Water shoes

What you’ll do

  • Halal buffet lunch on the beach at Naka Noi: fried chicken, chicken tom yum, stir-fried vegetables and fresh fruit
  • Snorkel in clear, shallow water
  • Relax on the beach in a beach chair
  • See the monkeys at Monkey Island
  • Plenty of photo stops along the way

Why we book with Phuket Jet Ski Club

  • Licensed guides in 5 languages: Thai, English, Russian, Chinese and Arabic
  • Small groups: one guide team for every 6 jet skis
  • GPS tracking on every jet ski, with a support speedboat on standby
  • Staff trained in first aid and CPR
  • Replacement jet ski within 10 minutes if yours has a problem
  • Jet ski damage insurance included, covering one incident per trip. If you hit another renter’s jet ski, you pay for that damage yourself.

The route can change because of tides and weather.
