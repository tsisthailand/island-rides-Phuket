Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost to rent a motorbike in Phuket?

At Island Rides in Chalong, a Honda Click 125 starts at 300 THB/day in low season (1 May – 31 Oct) and 400 THB/day in high season (1 Nov – 30 Apr). Big scooters like the Honda ADV 350 and Forza 350 start at 750 THB/day, and the Honda X-ADV 750 at 1,400 THB/day in low season. Monthly rates start at 4,500 THB.

Is the motorbike insured? What if I have an accident?

Our motorbikes have no insurance for damage to the bike. The compulsory insurance only covers the rider’s injuries (up to 30,000 THB). If the bike is damaged, the renter is fully responsible: you pay the actual repair bill plus the bike’s daily rental rate for every day it is in repair. If someone else hits you, call the police and call us straight away, and collect the cost of the damage from the other party on the spot. We can help assess the damage cost. See all motorbike rental terms →

What is included in the rental?

Every rental includes 2 free helmets, a free phone holder and compulsory insurance (rider injury only — the bike itself is not insured).

How much is the deposit?

Deposits are 4,000 THB or a passport for 125–155cc scooters, 5,000 THB or a passport for the PCX 160 and ADV 160, 7,000 THB or a passport for 350cc bikes. The X-ADV 750 needs a passport only (no cash deposit), in both seasons. The deposit is refunded within 24 hours after return if there is no damage or rule violation.

Is there a minimum rental period?

In high season (1 Nov – 30 Apr) the minimum rental is 3 days for all bikes. The Honda X-ADV 750 has a 3-day minimum all year. The longer you rent, the lower the daily rate; monthly and 3-month rates are available in low season.

Can I ride to Krabi or Phang Nga?

Only on 350cc and 750cc bikes: Phang Nga and Krabi are allowed with our approval first and an extra fee. Riding beyond Krabi has a 10,000 THB penalty. Bikes of 160cc and smaller must stay in Phuket. Leaving Phuket without approval has a 20,000 THB penalty.

Do you deliver the bike?

Yes, delivery and pick-up are available for an extra fee that depends on the area and bike size. It is free or reduced if you collect at our shop in Chalong.

How can I pay?

Cash, bank transfer or Wise, card (5% fee) or USDT (3% fee). Long-term rentals can be paid upfront or monthly.

What happens if I return the bike late?

Late return is 150 THB per hour. More than 4 hours late is charged as one extra day. Extensions must be requested at least 24 hours in advance.

Where is Island Rides located?

Our shop is at 7/10 Soi Ta-iad, Chalong, Mueang Phuket, Phuket 83130, Thailand. It is open Monday to Saturday 09:30–19:00 and Sunday 09:30–18:00. See it on Google Maps.

Can I rent a scooter monthly in Phuket?

Yes. In low season (1 May – 31 Oct) all our bikes have 30+ day and 3+ month rates, starting at 4,500 THB for a Honda Click 125, or 4,000 THB/month for 3+ months. In high season (1 Nov – 30 Apr) long rentals use our daily rates, which get lower the longer you rent. See the monthly scooter rental page for every model.

Is there a scooter rental near Soi Ta-iad in Chalong?

Yes. Island Rides is at 7/10 Soi Ta-iad, Chalong, Mueang Phuket 83130 (Plus Code V84V+G6), open Monday–Saturday 09:30–19:00 and Sunday 09:30–18:00. You can collect a scooter at the shop or ask for delivery for an extra fee. Directions on Google Maps.

What should I check when choosing a motorbike rental in Phuket?

Check the bike’s age and condition, the deposit (cash or passport), what insurance is included, where you are allowed to ride, and the late-return and damage rules. At Island Rides every rental includes 2 helmets, a phone holder and compulsory insurance (rider injury only — the bike itself is not insured), deposits are refunded within 24 hours after return if there is no damage, and all terms are listed on this page.
