Where you can ride

  • 160cc and smaller: Phuket island, up to 60km outside radius.
  • 350cc / 750cc only: Phang Nga and Krabi allowed, 146km maximum distance, extra fee applies.
  • Leaving Phuket without prior permission, or riding beyond Krabi, results in a fine — ask us first if you’re planning a longer trip.
  • No sub-renting, no riding under the influence of alcohol or drugs.

Policies

  • Rental fees are non-refundable. Deposit is refunded within 24 hours after return if there’s no damage or rule violation.
  • Late return: 150 THB/hour. More than 4 hours late is charged as one extra day.
  • Renter is responsible for damage/repair costs and towing, even if it exceeds the deposit.
  • Extensions must be requested at least 24 hours in advance to avoid a late penalty.

Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost to rent a motorbike in Phuket?

At Island Rides in Chalong, a Honda Click 125 starts at 300 THB/day in low season (1 May – 31 Oct) and 400 THB/day in high season (1 Nov – 30 Apr). Big scooters like the Honda ADV 350 and Forza 350 start at 750 THB/day, and the Honda X-ADV 750 at 1,400 THB/day in low season. Monthly rates start at 4,500 THB.

What is included in the rental?

Every rental includes 2 free helmets, a free phone holder and compulsory insurance.

How much is the deposit?

Deposits are 4,000 THB or a passport for 125–155cc scooters, 5,000 THB or a passport for the PCX 160 and ADV 160, 7,000 THB or a passport for 350cc bikes. The X-ADV 750 needs a passport in low season, and 20,000 THB or a passport in high season. The deposit is refunded within 24 hours after return if there is no damage or rule violation.

Is there a minimum rental period?

In high season (1 Nov – 30 Apr) the minimum rental is 3 days. The longer you rent, the lower the daily rate, with monthly and 3-month rates available.

Can I ride to Krabi or Phang Nga?

Only on 350cc and 750cc bikes: Phang Nga and Krabi are allowed up to 146 km, with an extra fee. Bikes of 160cc and smaller stay on Phuket island (up to 60 km outside radius). Leaving Phuket without permission results in a fine, so ask us first.

Do you deliver the bike?

Yes, delivery and pick-up are available for an extra fee that depends on the area and bike size. It is free or reduced if you collect at our shop in Chalong.

How can I pay?

Cash, bank transfer or Wise, card (5% fee) or USDT (3% fee). Long-term rentals can be paid upfront or monthly.

What happens if I return the bike late?

Late return is 150 THB per hour. More than 4 hours late is charged as one extra day. Extensions must be requested at least 24 hours in advance.

Where is Island Rides located?

Our shop is at 7 Chalong 10, Soi Ta-iad, Chalong, Mueang Phuket, Phuket 83130, Thailand. It is open Monday to Saturday 09:30–19:00 and Sunday 09:30–18:00. See it on Google Maps.

Can I rent a scooter monthly in Phuket?

Yes. All our bikes have 30+ day and 3+ month rates. In low season (1 May – 31 Oct) monthly rental starts at 4,500 THB for a Honda Click 125, or 4,000 THB/month for 3+ months. See the monthly scooter rental page for every model.
