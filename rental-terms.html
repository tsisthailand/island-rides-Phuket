Car Rental Terms

Please read these before you book. They match the rental agreement you sign at pick-up.

1. Payment & deposit

  • Rent is paid in full upfront, or monthly for long-term rentals.
  • A security deposit is required, in cash or by leaving your passport. The amount depends on the car (see each car on the car rental page).
  • The deposit is refunded within 24–72 hours after you return the car, if there is no damage or violation.
  • If you break this agreement, the deposit is not refunded.

2. Early return

  • You can return the car earlier than planned.
  • Rent already paid is not refunded, and unused days cannot be saved for a later rental.
  • Your deposit is returned as normal (if there is no damage or violation).

3. Cancelling a long-term contract

  • If you cancel a long-term (monthly) contract before the end of the agreed period, a penalty applies: the time you used the car is charged at our actual rental rate (not the discounted long-term rate).
  • The deposit is also kept.
Returning the car early (section 2) is different from cancelling a long-term contract (section 3). If you are not sure which applies to you, message us before you return the car.

4. Fuel

  • Use the correct fuel for your car: petrol (gasoline 95) for all Raptors, sedans and the Xpander, diesel for the Stormtrak, Wildtrak and Everest. Each car’s fuel type is shown on its listing.
  • Return the car with the same fuel level as at pick-up. Missing fuel is charged per fuel bar, from 500 THB up to 1,000 THB per bar depending on the size of the car.

5. Accidents & insurance

  • All cars have first-class insurance.
  • In any damage or accident, the renter pays the insurance excess, starting from 5,000 THB.
  • If the accident is your fault, you also pay the car’s daily rental rate while it is being repaired. If it is not your fault, there is no daily charge.
  • After an accident: do not move the car, follow police instructions, and contact us immediately.
  • The renter is fully responsible for negligence, accidents involving alcohol or drugs, and any traffic or legal violations.

6. Extra charges

  • Lost key: 6,000 THB
  • Normal cleaning (if returned very dirty): 1,000 THB
  • Smoking, weed or bad smell inside the car: 3,000 THB cleaning charge
  • Late return: 200 THB per hour. More than 6 hours late is charged as 1 full extra day.
  • Traffic fines and any damage are paid by the renter.

7. Using the car

  • Rental area: Phuket, Phang Nga and Krabi only. Driving outside these 3 provinces: 20,000 THB penalty. Crossing international borders is not allowed.
  • Personal use only. No sub-renting.
  • Only the renter may drive, and must hold a valid driving licence.
  • No smoking in the car.
  • You accept the car in its condition at pick-up, so please check it with us before you drive away.

8. Theft & tracking

  • If the car is stolen or written off, the renter pays its full market value.
  • Our cars are tracked. If this agreement is broken, we may locate and take back the car immediately.
Full terms are confirmed in the signed rental agreement at pick-up. Questions? Message us on WhatsApp before you book.
