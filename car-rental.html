What we offer

  • Cars available only from our own managed fleet — we don’t broker vehicles we can’t stand behind.
  • Airport or shop pick-up available.
  • Good for tourist trips, family travel, and long-stay rentals.
  • Daily, weekly and monthly rates, with lower prices in low season (1 May – 31 Oct).
  • Deposit: Raptor 20,000 THB or passport, Everest 10,000 THB or passport, Stormtrak 10,000 THB or passport, Xpander 7,000 THB, Ranger Wildtrak 7,000 THB, Civic 5,000 THB or passport, Almera 5,000 THB or passport, Yaris 5,000 THB. Refunded within 24 hours after return.

Where you can drive

  • Phuket, Phang Nga and Krabi only.
  • Driving outside these 3 provinces: 20,000 THB penalty. Crossing international borders is not allowed.
  • No sub-renting, no driving under the influence of alcohol or drugs.

Policies

  • Rental fees are non-refundable. Deposit refunded within 24–72 hours after return if there’s no damage or violation.
  • Early return is allowed: rent already paid is not refunded and unused days can’t be saved for later, but your deposit is returned.
  • Cancelling a long-term contract early: the time used is charged at our actual rental rate, and the deposit is kept.
  • Renter is responsible for full repair/replacement costs and towing, even if it exceeds the deposit.
  • Late monthly payment: 500 THB/day. 7+ days late allows us to end the contract and keep the deposit.

Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost to rent a Ford Ranger Raptor in Phuket?

Low season (1 May – 31 Oct): 2,500 THB/day, 18,500 THB/week or 60,000 THB/month. High season (1 Nov – 30 Apr): 3,000 THB/day, 20,000 THB/week or 70,000 THB/month. Deposit is 20,000 THB or your passport.

How much does it cost to rent a Ford Everest in Phuket?

Ford Everest 2021 (7 seats, diesel). Low season (1 May – 31 Oct): 1,500 THB/day, 10,000 THB/week or 30,000 THB/month. High season (1 Nov – 30 Apr): 2,000 THB/day, 14,000 THB/week or 50,000 THB/month. Deposit is 10,000 THB or your passport.

What is the cheapest car to rent in Phuket?

Toyota Yaris 2015 (5 seats, automatic). Low season (1 May – 31 Oct): 700 THB/day, 4,900 THB/week or 10,000 THB/month. High season (1 Nov – 30 Apr): 800 THB/day, 5,600 THB/week or 12,000 THB/month. Deposit is 5,000 THB.

What is included with the car?

Full first-class insurance, delivery around Phuket and 24/7 support. Airport or shop pick-up is available.

Can I drive to Krabi or Phang Nga?

Yes. Our cars may be driven in Phuket, Phang Nga and Krabi only. Driving outside these 3 provinces is not allowed and has a 20,000 THB penalty. Crossing international borders is not allowed.

What happens if I have an accident?
Do not move the car, follow police instructions and call us straight away. All cars have first-class insurance. Who pays depends on what happened:
  • Your fault, or no other vehicle involved: you pay the insurance excess (deductible), starting from 5,000 THB, plus the car’s daily rental rate for every day the car is being repaired and cannot be rented out, until the repair is finished.
  • The other party drives off or can’t be identified: you pay as above, unless you report it to the police and the other party is found, in which case they pay.
  • Another car or motorbike hits our car: their insurance pays. If they have no insurance, the other party pays the full cost, collected on the spot.
