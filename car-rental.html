What we offer

  • Cars available only from our own managed fleet — we don’t broker vehicles we can’t stand behind.
  • Airport or shop pick-up available.
  • Good for tourist trips, family travel, and long-stay rentals.
  • Daily, weekly and monthly rates, with lower prices in low season (1 May – 31 Oct).
  • Deposit: Raptor 20,000 THB, Everest 10,000 THB — or your passport. Refunded within 24 hours after return.

Where you can drive

  • Phang Nga and Krabi allowed, 146km maximum distance.
  • Want to go further in Thailand? Just let us know first. Crossing international borders is not allowed.
  • Fine for travel beyond Krabi without permission: 10,000 THB.
  • No sub-renting, no driving under the influence of alcohol or drugs.

Policies

  • Rental fees are non-refundable. Deposit refunded within 24 hours after return if there’s no damage or violation.
  • Renter is responsible for full repair/replacement costs and towing, even if it exceeds the deposit.
  • Monthly long-term rentals: early termination isn’t available — returning early forfeits the deposit.
  • Late monthly payment: 500 THB/day. 7+ days late allows us to end the contract and keep the deposit.
Full terms are confirmed in the signed rental agreement at pick-up.

Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost to rent a Ford Ranger Raptor in Phuket?

Low season (1 May – 31 Oct): 2,500 THB/day, 18,500 THB/week or 60,000 THB/month. High season (1 Nov – 30 Apr): 3,000 THB/day, 20,000 THB/week or 70,000 THB/month. Deposit is 20,000 THB or your passport.

How much does it cost to rent a Ford Everest in Phuket?

Ford Everest 2021 (7 seats, diesel). Low season (1 May – 31 Oct): 1,500 THB/day, 10,000 THB/week or 30,000 THB/month. High season (1 Nov – 30 Apr): 2,000 THB/day, 14,000 THB/week or 50,000 THB/month. Deposit is 10,000 THB or your passport.

What is the cheapest car to rent in Phuket?

Toyota Yaris 2015 (5 seats, automatic). Low season (1 May – 31 Oct): 700 THB/day, 2,800 THB/week or 10,000 THB/month. High season (1 Nov – 30 Apr): 800 THB/day, 5,600 THB/week or 12,000 THB/month.

What is included with the car?

Full first-class insurance, delivery around Phuket and 24/7 support. Airport or shop pick-up is available.

Can I drive to Krabi or Phang Nga?

Yes. Phang Nga and Krabi are allowed up to 146 km. If you want to go further in Thailand, let us know first. Crossing international borders is not allowed, and travel beyond Krabi without permission has a 10,000 THB fine.

Can I end a monthly rental early?

No. Early termination is not available on monthly long-term rentals, and returning early forfeits the deposit.
