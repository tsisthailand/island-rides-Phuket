Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost to rent a Ford Ranger Raptor in Phuket?

Low season (1 May – 31 Oct): 2,500 THB/day, 18,500 THB/week or 60,000 THB/month. High season (1 Nov – 30 Apr): 3,000 THB/day, 20,000 THB/week or 70,000 THB/month. Deposit is 20,000 THB or your passport.

What insurance does the Ranger Raptor have?

It comes with full first-class insurance, plus 24/7 support during your rental.

Do you deliver the Raptor?

Yes. Delivery around Phuket is included, and airport or shop pick-up is available.

Can I drive the Raptor to Krabi or Phang Nga?

Yes. Our cars may be driven in Phuket, Phang Nga and Krabi only. Driving outside these 3 provinces is not allowed and has a 20,000 THB penalty. Crossing international borders is not allowed.

Can I rent the Raptor monthly?

Yes: 60,000 THB/month in low season or 70,000 THB/month in high season. You can return the car early (rent is not refunded, deposit is returned). Cancelling a monthly contract early is charged at our actual rental rate and the deposit is kept. Late monthly payment is 500 THB/day.
