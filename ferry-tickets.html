Frequently asked questions

Where does the ferry leave from in Phuket?

Ferries and speedboats to Phi Phi leave from Rassada Pier in Phuket town. Hotel transfer to Rassada Pier is included in our ferry ticket rates.

How long does it take to get to Phi Phi?

About 2 hours by ferry and about 1 hour by speedboat.

What is not included in the price?

Island fees and national park fees are not included and are paid on the day.

Are children cheaper?

Yes, child prices apply for ages 4–11.
