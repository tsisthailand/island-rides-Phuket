🆕 New to driving in Thailand? Get your new Thai license

A complete, guided service for foreigners who don’t hold a driving license yet — from registration all the way to collecting your card. Transport for all 3 days is included, with pickup from an agreed meeting point.

  1. Day 1 — Fingerprint registration at the driving school (about 2 hours). Trips run every Thursday, or Wednesday for combined motorbike + car applications.
  2. Day 2 — Saturday at the driving school (full day): a short video session, a multiple-choice test and your practical driving test. Lunch included.
  3. Day 3 — Collect your new Thai driving license card at the transport office (about 2 hours).

What to bring:

  • Passport
  • Medical certificate — we can arrange it for you (extra fee)
  • Residence certificate from Immigration — we can arrange it for you (extra fee)
  • TM30 (notification of residence) — we can arrange it for you (extra fee)

Frequently asked questions

Can a foreigner get a Thai driving license?

Yes. If you don’t have a license yet, we guide you through a new Thai license in 3 days. If you already have a license from home, we can convert it to a Thai license in a single appointment day.

What do I need to convert my license?

Your original passport, your original driving license from home, a medical certificate, a certificate of residence and a TM30 form. We can arrange the medical certificate, residence certificate and TM30 for you for an extra fee.

Does my license need to be translated?

Depending on your nationality, your license may need to be translated and legalized at your consulate first. We check this for you right away, so there are no surprises later.

How long does it take?

New license: 3 days (registration, test day, and collecting your card). Conversion: about 2 hours on one appointment day, and your card is issued the same day. VIP service: about 2 hours, same day.

Is transport included?

Yes. Transport to and from the offices on your appointment days is included, with pickup from an agreed meeting point.

How do I pay?

The full service fee is paid upfront, and we sign a service agreement together so you have everything in writing as your guarantee. The government card fee is paid in cash on collection day (included in the VIP service). Message us for current prices.

Licenses are issued by the Thai Department of Land Transport according to its current requirements. We help with documents, appointments and transport.
