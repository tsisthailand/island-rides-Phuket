Frequently asked questions

How much does a monthly scooter rental cost in Phuket?

At Island Rides in Chalong, low season (1 May – 31 Oct) monthly rental starts at 4,500 THB for a Honda Click 125 (30+ days) and 4,000 THB/month for 3+ months. A Honda PCX 160 or ADV 160 is 7,500 THB, and a Honda ADV 350 from 13,000 THB. In high season (1 Nov – 30 Apr) a Click 125 is 5,000 THB/month and an ADV 350 is 19,000 THB/month.

Is the motorbike insured? What if I have an accident?

Our motorbikes have no insurance for damage to the bike. The compulsory insurance only covers the rider’s injuries (up to 30,000 THB). If the bike is damaged, the renter is fully responsible: you pay the actual repair bill plus the bike’s daily rental rate for every day it is in repair. If someone else hits you, call the police and call us straight away, and collect the cost of the damage from the other party on the spot. We can help assess the damage cost. Is the 3-month rate cheaper than the monthly rate?

Yes, in low season. For example the Honda Click 125 is 4,500 THB for 30+ days and 4,000 THB/month for 3+ months, and the Honda ADV 350 (2024–2025) is 13,000 THB and 12,000 THB/month.

Can I pay monthly instead of upfront?

Yes. Long-term rentals can be paid upfront or monthly, by cash, bank transfer or Wise, card (5% fee) or USDT (3% fee).

How much is the deposit for a monthly rental?

It depends on the bike: 4,000 THB or a passport for 125–155cc scooters, 5,000 THB or a passport for the PCX 160 and ADV 160, and 7,000 THB or a passport for 350cc bikes in low season. The X-ADV 750 needs a passport in low season and 20,000 THB or a passport in high season. The deposit is refunded within 24 hours after return if there is no damage or rule violation.

What is included in a monthly rental?

Every rental includes 2 free helmets, a free phone holder and compulsory insurance (rider injury only; the bike itself is not insured), plus after-sales support from our shop in Chalong during your rental.

Can I extend my rental?

Yes. Please request an extension at least 24 hours in advance to avoid a late penalty. Late return is 150 THB per hour, and more than 4 hours late is charged as one extra day.

Which scooter is best for a month in Phuket?

The Honda Click 125 and Click 160 are light and easy for town and the beaches. The Honda PCX 160 is smooth and roomy and a favourite for monthly rentals. For two people or longer rides, the Honda ADV 350, Forza 350 or Yamaha XMAX are bigger and more comfortable, and 350cc bikes may ride to Phang Nga and Krabi with an extra fee.

Where is Island Rides located?

Our shop is at 7/10 Soi Ta-iad, Chalong, Mueang Phuket, Phuket 83130, Thailand. It is open Monday to Saturday 09:30–19:00 and Sunday 09:30–18:00. See it on Google Maps.
