Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost to rent a Honda X-ADV 750 in Phuket?

In low season (1 May – 31 Oct) the 2024 X-ADV 750 is 1,400 THB/day for 1–6 days and 28,000 THB for 30+ days; the 2025 model is 1,500 THB/day and the 2026 model 1,600 THB/day. In high season (1 Nov – 30 Apr) it is 2,500 THB/day for 3 days, down to 45,000 THB/month.

Is the motorbike insured? What if I have an accident?

Our motorbikes have no insurance for damage to the bike. The compulsory insurance only covers the rider’s injuries (up to 30,000 THB). If the bike is damaged, the renter is fully responsible: you pay the actual repair bill plus the bike’s daily rental rate for every day it is in repair. If someone else hits you, call the police and call us straight away, and collect the cost of the damage from the other party on the spot. We can help assess the damage cost. How much is the deposit for the X-ADV 750?

In low season, your passport only. In high season, 20,000 THB or your passport. The deposit is refunded within 24 hours after return if there is no damage or rule violation.

Do all your X-ADV 750s have an Akrapovic exhaust?

Yes. Every X-ADV 750 in our fleet is fitted with an Akrapovic exhaust.

Can I ride the X-ADV 750 to Krabi or Phang Nga?

Yes. 350cc and 750cc bikes may go to Phang Nga and Krabi with our approval first and an extra fee. Riding beyond Krabi has a 10,000 THB penalty, and leaving Phuket without approval has a 20,000 THB penalty.

Can I rent the X-ADV 750 monthly?

Yes. Monthly and 3-month rates are available, and the longer you rent, the lower the daily rate.

At our shop at 7/10 Soi Ta-iad, Chalong, Mueang Phuket, Phuket 83130, Thailand, open Monday–Saturday 09:30–19:00 and Sunday 09:30–18:00. Delivery is available for an extra fee.
