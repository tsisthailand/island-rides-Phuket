📚 1-Year Thai ED Visa (Phuket)

Study Thai or English with a partner school and stay in Thailand for 12 months.

  • School enrolment includes: 80 hours of Thai or English classes, full 12-month enrolment, and visa document preparation and processing.
  • Step 1 — Enrol: pay the school fee. Classes may start the same week.
  • Step 2 — About 7 days later: tourist visa is converted to a Non-ED visa at Immigration.
  • Step 3 — Every 90 days: visa extension (3 times during the year).

Notes:

  • Classes may start the same week, based on the agreed schedule.
  • Immigration fees depend on your age and passport nationality — we confirm the full cost with you before you pay.
  • You need at least 30 days left on your current visa when applying; otherwise extend first.
  • No refund once classes have started. Cancel within 5 days of payment (before classes start) for an 80% refund; after that, no refund.
  • Visa approval is decided by Immigration, not the school. Extra documents may be requested, and refusal for missing documents is not refunded.

Frequently asked questions

How long does a visa extension take?

Usually same-day or next-day. We prepare and check your documents and submit the extension at Immigration for you, for all visa types.

How does the 1-year ED visa work?

You enrol with a partner school for Thai or English classes (80 hours over 12 months). About 7 days later your tourist visa is converted to a Non-ED visa, then extended every 90 days. Message us for the current price for your age and nationality.

Can you help with a retirement visa?

Yes. The retirement visa is for foreigners aged 50 or over and needs proof of income or savings and health insurance. Message us and we’ll show you how to qualify financially and what documents you need.

Where can I do a border run from Phuket?

We run same-day return trips to Ranong (Myanmar, with a short boat crossing) and Satun (Malaysia, overland). Hotel pickup from 5:00 AM, back in Phuket the same evening.

What do I need for a border run?

A passport valid for at least 6 months and 2 passport-sized photos. You also need the Thailand Digital Arrival Card (TDAC). Land border runs are limited to 2 times per calendar year.

Do you guarantee my visa will be approved?

No. Visa approval is decided by Thai Immigration, not by us or the school. We prepare your documents carefully and tell you honestly what is needed, but Immigration may ask for more documents.

Stamped Visa Service is an independent visa service agent, not part of Thai Immigration. Visa approval and fees are decided by Immigration and can change; prices may vary depending on your case and documents.
